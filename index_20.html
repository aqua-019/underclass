That lands within about five percent of stonkfun's total, and it lands under, which is the direction an honest error should go: a holder who has since sold and closed their token account stops looking like a holder. The obvious objections

Is this affiliated with Anthropic or Dario Amodei?

No. Not in any sense, at any level, in either direction. We quote a widely reported public warning and link the source. Anthropic has not endorsed this, has not been asked to, and would almost certainly decline. The $ANTHROPIC pre-stock pairing is a market on a third-party launchpad — not a relationship, a partnership, or a claim on the company.

How do I know the PreStock actually reached me?

You don't have to know — you can look. The wallet checker on this page reads your two balances straight off Solana and prices them against the open market, and it will show you a zero just as readily as a number. Nothing about your position depends on a figure we publish; it depends on what a public RPC returns for your address. If the checker and your wallet disagree, believe your wallet and tell us.

What is Anthropic pre-stock, exactly?

A tokenized claim tracking pre-IPO Anthropic exposure, traded on Solana as Pren1Fv…5Lkhw under the Token-2022 program. It is not equity. It carries no shareholding, no voting right, no dividend and no relationship with Anthropic, who have not endorsed any of this and would almost certainly decline if asked. It trades in a thin market, it can gap, and the price the checker shows is the deepest live pool at that second — not a valuation.

Is this the official escapethepermanentunderclass.com?

No. That site is by @rameerez and predates this token by roughly six months. We inherited the countdown and the thesis the way anyone inherits a meme — by finding it useful. If the original author objects, we'll say so here.

Why is a coin about being poor priced in pre-IPO stock?

Because that is genuinely where the deepest book is, and because the joke writes itself. It trades against $ANTHROPIC pre-stock on Raydium rather than against SOL. The thing you buy to escape the underclass is denominated in the thing the underclass is supposedly being created by. We didn't plan this. We're refusing to fix it.

What actually happens on 1 January 2028?

Nothing. No launch, no legislation, no economic event. The date is a forecast, not an appointment — closer to a hurricane cone than a train timetable. Anyone selling you certainty about that date, a countdown clock included, is selling you something.

Isn't the counter-argument that all of this is wrong?

Yes, and it's a decent one — which is why four of the nine sources above argue against us. Jevons' paradox says every efficiency shock in history increased consumption of whatever it made cheap. Baumol says a productivity boom in one sector lifts wages across all of them. Acemoglu's version is sharpest: an underclass is a policy outcome, not a law of physics. We think the risk is real. We don't think it's settled.

Isn't selling a token about the underclass a bit rich?

Every apocalypse arrives with a gift shop. Notice who profits from your urgency, this website included. What we won't do is dress a memecoin as a retirement plan. Buy it because the joke lands, size it like a joke, and put the serious money somewhere serious.
