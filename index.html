07 — The lore

How a group chat
joke became
a deadline.

The phrase was not issued by a press office. It escaped from one timeline into another and then hardened into a worldview — which is the only way anything gets believed now.

May 2025
The warning gets a name attached

Anthropic's chief executive tells Axios that AI could eliminate half of all entry-level white-collar jobs and push unemployment to 10–20% inside one to five years, leaving an unemployed or very-low-wage underclass. He is not describing a fear. He is describing his own product's second-order effects, unprompted.

8 Oct 2025
The New Yorker names the meme

Kyle Chayka's column, “Will A.I. Trap You in the ‘Permanent Underclass’?”, calls the phrase “modern online parlance” and notes it was already circulating among people in Silicon Valley who expect much of human labour to become economically obsolete. Chayka's read: the fear exists because nobody has a coherent picture of how an AI-dominated society is meant to be structured.

27 Oct 2025
NPR finds it in the wild

The Indicator covers it as an existing X meme, reporting posts along the lines of “we only have five months to escape the permanent underclass.” Confirmation it grew in the timeline rather than in a keynote — no press office, no launch, no owner.

Through 2025
2027 becomes the load-bearing year

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness and the forecasting scene around it fix on 2027 as the inflection. The meme borrows the date the way memes always borrow: without asking.

11 Feb 2026
A poster

Javi Rameerez makes one. That is the entire origin event — no thread, no deck, no launch.

12 Feb 2026
The poster becomes a clock

His public work-in-progress log shows him building escapethepermanentunderclass.com out of the previous day's poster: a countdown, a thesis, an action list, and a wall of quotes from the people building the thing. The deadline is dated the same day — 31 December 2027, from a model run over 237 datapoints.

May 2026
It becomes a position you have to hold

The New York Times frames the “San Francisco consensus” in the paper of record. At this point it is no longer a joke with a clock attached; it is an argument people are expected to have an opinion about at dinner.

8 Aug 2026
$UNDERCLASS opens on stonk.fun

The token launches and pairs against $ANTHROPIC pre-stock — so the escape coin is quoted in a claim on the company that supplied the warning. Unaffiliated with every party named above, and funnier for it.

31 Dec 2027
The clock reaches zero

And then nothing happens, precisely on schedule. The date was never the point. The direction was.
